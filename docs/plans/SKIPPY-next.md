## Rulings that bind every step
1. **One front door, the same functions (D-137).** Skippy and agents call the same server functions as the normal pages: `db.createCompanyReferralRequest` for an ask, the quote route's math for a cost, `planCatalogResponse` for prices, and `startKitPackCheckout` for a pack. A second copy of any of them is a defect, not a shortcut (SKIPPY-fix S-1 was one).
2. **Flags.** Each step has its own off-by-default flag plus an account allow-list (the S-4 helper, `server/skippieAllowList.ts`). Production stays off until the owner's word after the `docs/SKIPPY.md` pre-launch checklist. Real money only on the Chargebee test site until the owner's one-word yes (D-146, D-065).
3. **State.** The chat session and Skippy cards live in process memory (as in steps 2 and 4). A lost one only means asking again: it never loses money, because the ledger, payment intents and asks are durable. Anything a later step needs to survive a restart (an approval waiting for the seeker, an auto-send setting) goes in a table.
4. **Copy (D-153).** Every line says the action, the outcome and the cost plainly, promises only what works today, writes "skipwait.me", and uses no long dashes.

## SKIPPY-3: buy a credit pack inside Skippy (lane `skippy-seeker`, Tier 1, `todo`)
**Why.** Today a seeker short of credits at the quote step is sent away ("Add credits on /plans first", `server/skippieSeeker.ts:207`, `:391`, `:397`) and loses the chat. Build step 3 of D-136: choose credits in the chat, approve a money card, pay on Chargebee's page, come back and send.
**Rulings.**
- **What is sold:** credit packs only, the ones `planCatalogResponse` marks available for the seeker's region (D-146: packs are on sale; passes are off, `PASS_SALES_ENABLED = false`; the D-132 plans are off). When the owner turns plans on, they join through the same card in a later row, not this one.
- **Which packs are offered:** up to 3 available packs, smallest first, whose credits cover the shortfall from the fresh quote. If none covers it, offer the largest available pack and say plainly that the ask still needs more credits.
- **The money card (D-140):** it shows the pack name, the credits it adds, the exact amount and currency from the catalog (never hardcoded), and one undo line. The undo line is the wording `/plans` already shows for packs; if `/plans` shows none, it reads "Payment happens on Chargebee's secure page. Refunds follow the refund policy." with a link to the refund page. Nothing new is promised. The card is one-use, expires in 10 minutes and belongs to the asking account. It is bound to `{userId, packId, billingCountry, amount, currency, credits}`, and the client sends only the card id.
- **Approve** (`POST /api/skippie/cards/:id/approve`, same flag and allow-list as `/api/skippie/*`): re-read the catalog. If the pack, amount, currency or credits changed, or the pack is no longer available, refuse with "The price changed. Nothing was charged." and offer a fresh card. Otherwise mark the card used, then run the same code as the kit-pack arm of `POST /api/chargebee/checkout` (`server/chargebeeRoutes.ts:101-115`). Extract that arm into one exported function, used by the route and by Skippy, that takes the return URLs as input: the referrer refusal first (D-055), `startKitPackCheckout`, `createPaymentIntent`, the `billing.credit_checkout_started` row. The route keeps its `/plans` URLs, and Skippy passes `/skippy?payment=pending` and `/skippy?payment=cancelled`. Respond with the `checkoutUrl`, which the client opens. Skippy never charges anything itself.
- **Back in Skippy:** on `payment=pending`, run the same recovery the `/plans` page runs on return (`client/src/components/plans/PendingCheckoutReturn.tsx` and its pack path; reuse it, never re-implement it). Re-fetch the credit summary until it changes or the bounded poll ends, then re-quote and continue at the `quote` step with the confirmed note. On `payment=cancelled` or a declined payment, say "Payment didn't go through. Nothing was charged." and stay on the quote.
- **Fresh sign-in (D-140 "money needs a fresh re-login"):** the step-up is its own row, SKIPPY-stepup, below. SKIPPY-3 is built and proven on the test site without it, behind its own flag `SKIPPIE_PURCHASE_ENABLED` (off; `.env.example`, `src/worker.ts`, config contract). The flag may not go on in production until SKIPPY-stepup has landed.

**Red-first tests.** A tampered amount, currency or pack in the approve body is ignored, because only the card id counts. A second approve of the same card is refused, and only one payment intent exists. An expired card is refused. Another account's card id gets 404. A price change between card and approve is refused with no checkout started. A referrer-role call is refused before any provider call (D-055). A declined or cancelled return says nothing was charged. A webhook redelivery for a Skippy-started checkout credits once, through the existing path (`payment-credit-<id>`). The quote after a credited pack lets the seeker send.
**Owned paths.** `server/skippieSeeker.ts` (quote step and two new turn kinds: `buy_credits` and `choose_pack`), `server/skippieSeekerRoutes.ts` (the card route), `server/skippieMoneyCards.ts` (new), `server/chargebeeRoutes.ts` (extract the kit-pack arm only, behaviour unchanged, its existing tests stay green), `server/kitCheckout.ts` (the extracted function), `client/src/pages/Skippy.tsx` (card, return handling), `.env.example`, `src/worker.ts`, and the tests beside each.
**Proof.** On the Chargebee test site with the test seeker: shortfall, then card, then hosted page (test card), then back in Skippy with the credits added, then the ask sent; ledger rows for the purchase and the spend; a cancelled checkout. Captures at 1280 and 390. Never a real payment.

## SKIPPY-stepup: a fresh sign-in before a money card (lane `auth-stepup`, Tier 1, `architect`)
**Ruling.** A Skippy money card's Approve requires that the seeker's session was created by an interactive sign-in no more than 15 minutes earlier. A silent renewal does not count (`server/_core/sdk.ts:51-53` renews sessions). If the sign-in is older, Approve answers 401 `reauth_required` and changes nothing. The client sends the seeker through their normal sign-in (D-030/D-049: social or password, both free; never a new paid email per purchase where a free method exists) and back to the same card, if it has not expired. The check is server-side and lives in one function the money card calls.
**The packet must settle**, with file:line evidence: how the interactive sign-in time is carried for both auth planes (the `app_session_id` cookie JWT and the WorkOS bearer token), and how it survives renewal without being refreshed by it. It must also settle how WorkOS is asked to re-prompt, and the tests: an old session refused, a fresh one passed, a renewed old one refused, and both planes covered. Login-rule pins (`client/src/pages/loginRule.test.tsx`, `signIn.test.tsx`) stay green. Referrer sign-in is untouched.

## SKIPPY-6: agents send, with the seeker approving every send (lane `skippy-agents`, Tier 1, `todo`)
**Why.** Step 6 of D-136 and D-141 to D-142. Step 5 lets an agent draft but never send.
**Rulings.**
- **Scope:** the grant gains a `send` scope, off by default; the seeker turns it on when granting (`createAgentGrantInputSchema`). An agent with `send` may only *request* a send: `POST /api/agent/v1/drafts/:id/send-request`. The draft moves to `pending_approval` and nothing else happens. The seeker gets an in-app notice and one transactional email with a direct link (D-060: at most one such email per person per day; preferences respected).
- **The approval card, in the seeker's session** (`/agents`, and inside Skippy when open), shows the company, job link, note, the fresh quoted cost and the balance. The seeker chooses the resume (default: their most recent), and may edit the note: the note sent is byte-for-byte the one shown at approve (D-139). Approve calls `POST /api/seeker/agents/drafts/:id/approve` with the quoted cost. Decline sets `declined`. A draft waiting 72 hours expires to `declined` with nothing spent.
- **Approve, server side, in this order and in one transaction for the limit check:** check the grant is still owned, unrevoked, unexpired, unpaused and has `send`. Lock the grant row (`SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`) and count this grant's sent asks and their credits for the day and the week, so concurrent approvals cannot pass the limits (SKIPPY-fix noted the step 5 race). Re-quote, and refuse if the cost differs from the one approved (same rule as S-3). Then call `db.createCompanyReferralRequest(userId, { selectedCompany: <directory entry for companySlug>, targetRoleUrl, attachmentIds: [chosen], personalPitch: note, idempotencyKey: "agent-draft-<draftId>" })`. The `selectedCompany` check makes a link that does not belong to the company fail exactly as on the company page. The idempotency key makes a retried approve one ask. On success the draft becomes `sent` with its `requestId`, and every step writes a `seekerAgentEvents` row.
- **Never buys:** a shortfall leaves the draft pending with "needs credits" and a link to `/plans` for the seeker. No agent route reaches checkout (the existing test stays).
- **D-142 label:** add a table `askOrigins` (`referralRequestId` primary key, `channel` enum `skippy` or `agent`, `agentGrantId` nullable), written in the approve flow after the ask is created (and by Skippy's send for `skippy`, for analytics only). The referrer ask view (`shared/referrerAskView.ts`, `server/referrerAskView.ts`) adds `sentViaAssistant: true` only for `agent`, and `/queue` shows "Sent via the seeker's assistant" on that ask. Nothing else changes for the referrer: no name or resume before accept (D-019(e)). Credits, hold and refund follow the ask's normal rules.
- **Migration:** additive, information_schema-guarded, the next free number on `main` at landing (0108 is reserved for ROW20-CD's thank-you), recorded in plan §5. It holds the `askOrigins` table, `seekerAgentGrants.canSend` (default false) and the draft statuses `pending_approval`, `sent`, `declined`, plus `requestId`. Mirror it in `server/schemaReconcile.ts`.
- **Flag:** `SKIPPIE_AGENT_SEND_ENABLED` (off), on top of `SKIPPIE_AGENT_ENABLED` and its allow-list.

**Red-first tests.** A send-request without the `send` scope gets 403. Approve by another account gets 404. Revoke, pause or expiry between the request and approve refuses with nothing spent. Two concurrent approves over the daily limit produce one ask. A cost change refuses. A link outside the company's domains refuses. A retried approve gives one ask and one debit. Decline and the 72-hour expiry spend nothing. The referrer sees the label on an agent ask and not on a page or Skippy ask. A waiting (uncovered) company holds at no cost, as any ask does. The agent key never reaches a checkout route.
**Owned paths.** `server/seekerAgentRoutes.ts`, `server/seekerAgentDeps.ts`, `shared/seekerAgents.ts`, `drizzle/schema.ts` (the new table and columns), `drizzle/deploy/<next>_skippy_agent_send.sql`, `server/schemaReconcile.ts`, `shared/referrerAskView.ts` and `server/referrerAskView.ts` (one field), the `/queue` ask row (the label only), `client/src/pages/SeekerAgents.tsx`, `.env.example`, `src/worker.ts`, and the tests beside each.
**Proof.** With the test seeker on `skipwait_qa`: an agent drafts and requests a send, the seeker approves in `/agents`, the ask reaches `/queue` with the label, and the ledger shows one debit. Also show a decline and a revoke mid-flight. Captures at 1280 and 390.

## SKIPPY-7: opt-in auto-send within limits (lane `skippy-agents`, Tier 1, `architect`, after SKIPPY-6)
**Rulings for its packet.** Auto-send is a per-grant setting, `autoSend`, default off. Only the seeker can turn it on, in their session, on a confirmation that restates the grant's limits and expiry. It needs the `send` scope. An auto-send runs the exact SKIPPY-6 approve function with the actor recorded as `agent_auto`: same transaction, same limits, same cost check, same label. Revoke and pause stop it at the very next call, because both are checked inside the transaction. It never buys credits: a shortfall parks the draft for the seeker. The seeker gets one daily digest of what was auto-sent (D-060's one-email-a-day cap), and every send is in the audit log. Expiry stays within 30 days (D-141). The packet adds its own flag (`SKIPPIE_AGENT_AUTOSEND_ENABLED`, off) and names the files once SKIPPY-6 has landed.
