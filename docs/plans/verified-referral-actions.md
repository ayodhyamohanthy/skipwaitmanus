The owner's new direction is to charge seekers credits for verified introductions or agent outreach, and let referrers earn credits or cash only for verified work, including confirmed introductions and actual reviews within 24 hours. Agents should prepare useful candidate briefing packets for insiders rather than leave people on passive waitlists. This authorizes planning that direction, not an invented rate card or a silent change to existing asks. The owner subsequently authorized the plan and a separate phase-1 builder to proceed in parallel, without waiting for plan review to start building. The builder still needs a separately registered, claimed implementation packet. Independent review and gates are required before merge. Parallel build authorization does not approve this plan or permit billing activation.

The strongest objection is incentive fraud. Two people can attest to an introduction that never happened, and paid fast reviews can become hurried rubber stamps. The first release should test evidence quality without moving money. A click, email open, generated draft, accepted ask, elapsed timer or model confidence score is not proof of useful work. Neither employment nor an interview is guaranteed. Employer referral policies may prohibit personal rewards even when local law permits them; those cases must remain ineligible.

The seeker gets a cost tied to a defined action and an explanation of what was verified. The employee gets a short, relevant packet and can decline without penalty. The company gets consented, job-specific submissions rather than bulk unsolicited traffic. These benefits are hypotheses, not measured outcomes. The cheapest test is a synthetic shadow run followed by a small consented pilot after its own approval. The likely failure is apparent activity without useful introductions; compare independently confirmed actions, disputes and unsolicited-contact complaints, not clicks. Private progress and fair receipts should motivate useful work; public rankings or time pressure should not. Ship only if the pilot helps all three sides.

The ask's unique seeker/idempotency key and submission fingerprint protect repeat submission. An uncovered ask waits without a debit; deliverWaitingAskTx locks the request, profile, slots and wallet, then spends with askDeliveryKey when coverage becomes available. A debitTransactionId avoids a second delivery debit. Insufficient credits hold delivery without allocating or notifying a referrer. The code computes deliveryCost with askCreditCost from the stored job row at delivery; do not claim every waiting ask already has an immutable level quote. A VRA quote snapshot is new work.

D-135 says one credit per ask remains live until ASK_LEVEL_COST and its client flag are enabled. PLAN_FAMILY_V2 is separately gated. With level pricing active the intended amount is the job level, not a new VRA rate. Existing pricing approvals do not settle VRA prices. server/db.ts passReferralRequestTx currently reopens the ask, records the reason and releases access and allocation; it does not implement the pending D-035 reason-based credit refund. Review the actual merged D-035 result again before designing any bridge. Never rely on this snapshot to declare another lane fixed or live.

server/creditLedger.ts spendCreditsTx requires a locked wallet, locks grants after the wallet, rejects a shortfall as a whole, uses compare-and-set updates, and appends one debit per bucket allocation. shared/creditSpend.ts orders spendable buckets by earliest expiry, then promo, carryover, trial, cycle, welcome and purchased, with oldest grant breaking a tie. Purchased balance has no expiry. This is not FIFO across all purchased and earned balances. The caller supplies app time. Each allocation adds a numbered suffix to the reference and idempotency key.

drizzle/schema.ts tokenTransactions uniquely constrains debit references by user, role, kind, reference type and reference ID; idempotency is scoped by user, role, key and kind; reversesTransactionId is unique. server/db.ts refundLedgerSpendTx reads referral_request spend references numbered 1 through 32, skips already reversed rows and restores each eligible source once. Purchased credits return; monthly credits return only in the same cycle up to its allowance; promo and plan grants require the matching still-valid grant. A zero-return reversal records an ended source rather than manufacturing replacement credits. Withdraw-before-pickup and unclaimed expiry use this path. D-135's future non-expiring paid-credit rule must be verified separately at enable time. Do not expand this helper's reference namespace and assume it refunds VRA actions.

REFERRER_ACCEPT_REWARDS_ENABLED is false in server/db.ts. Existing legacy referrer wallets and invitation reward tables are not a payout account and must not be repurposed merely because they contain reward-related names. Payment fulfillment and refund recovery are separate from referral-action settlement. Chargebee purchase credits, unused-credit revocation and requires_review recovery remain untouched; read docs/PAYMENTS_TEST_RUNBOOK.md before an eventual integration. The proposed policy is vra-shadow-v1 for the first phase and a separately approved vra-actions-v1 for any eventual funded pilot. Existing asks remain visibility-v1, including their debit, release, refund and retry semantics. Never relabel a live ask in place, debit it again after an introduction, or grandfather away its refund. For a new VRA ask, show a versioned quote with action type, credit amount, evidence standard, expiry and maximum total before consent. Reserve the quoted amount at authorization, and capture it once only after that exact action verifies. No capture at visibility or pickup for that cohort. Insufficient funds reject the action authorization rather than create a credit debt. No unconsented repricing or automatic second bill.

Default proposal for the eventual pilot is one seeker-funded unit per consented action, with an introduction and its supporting review treated as one bundle unless the seeker explicitly purchases distinct services. A review reward may be an internal allocation of that bundle, not another seeker debit. Agent outreach is a separate, explicitly selected service only when its quote says so; the outreach send that produces the same paid introduction must not charge twice under two labels. A globally unique economic unit ties the action, recipient, request revision and quote together. Retries, provider callbacks and agent aliases all resolve to that unit. Concrete prices, whether a review alone is billable, reward funding and maximum rewards remain acceptance questions for the activating decision, not defaults hidden in code.

A review qualifies only after an authenticated, currently verified employee actually assesses the consented materials and submits a structured job-specific attestation. The 24-hour interval starts at that employee's accepted assignment after materials are available, ends at the attested review time, and uses server app time in UTC. Record assignment, material version and completion time. Deadline expiry means missed, never automatically reviewed or payable. A truthful pass can be a completed review if the approved offer permits it; a favorable opinion is never required. Repeated generic text, implausibly quick completion or contradictory receipts enters manual review. Timeliness is a metric or eligibility condition, never a promise of a referral.

A confirmed introduction requires an authorized recipient/employer-system receipt or independent recipient confirmation tied to this candidate, role and action. The referrer's own assertion alone is claimed, not verified. Agent outreach requires authorized dispatch and a verified delivery receipt or recipient acknowledgement that matches the destination and payload digest. A generated draft and a provider merely accepting a queue item are insufficient. A bounce, revoked consent, suppressed recipient or unknown final delivery stays unverified. Use an adapter with bounded requests, authenticated callbacks and deduplicated provider receipt IDs in a later packet, not an invented provider integration here.

Evidence must state what it proves. An immutable envelope records event ID, economic unit ID, request revision, quote and policy version, actor and subject references, verifier identity and method version, observed/server-received times, consent version, packet digest, scoped receipt reference, outcome and limitations. Preserve provenance through authenticated server collection; client timestamps, screenshots and signatures supplied by the beneficiary are claims unless independently corroborated. Record confidence as an explanation of evidence quality, never a threshold that magically proves an introduction. Exclude raw resumes, destination addresses, private messages and bearer links from the ledger or public issue.

The verifier must be distinct from the earning actor and may not be the seeking actor merely approving their friend's reward. Server-validated provider evidence or a separately authorized reviewer can corroborate a claim. Record relationships and conflicts, restrict reviewer permissions, audit overrides and require a second reviewer for overrides affecting money. Independent verification reduces collusion; it cannot prove its absence. Ambiguous evidence remains pending or rejected, not paid because a deadline passed.

The action state model separates proposed, authorized, reserved, evidence_pending, verification_pending, verified, settlement_pending and settled. Rejected, cancelled and expired are non-settling outcomes. A dispute can freeze an unsettled action; an already settled one becomes disputed without deleting its settlement. An upheld dispute closes with a recorded reason; an overturned action appends a reversal and a superseding verification decision. Evidence and decisions are append-only. Status projections may change under revision checks, but prior facts remain inspectable.

Future persistence would introduce policy/quote snapshots, scoped consents, action instances, immutable evidence, verification decisions, reservations, settlement links and disputes. Keep credits and cash in different ledgers and units. A credit reward grant would need an explicit source, expiry/transfer rules, funding cap and keyed grant path; it is not a wallet cash liability. A cash reward, if later approved, needs a separately funded payable, currency, tax/compliance review, employer-policy eligibility and payout reconciliation. No cash wallet or provider payout is in phases 1 or 2, and earned credits never auto-convert to cash.

Reservation support does not exist merely because spendCreditsTx exists. A later money-core packet must make every spending path honor outstanding holds, including ordinary asks, paid tools, cycle reconciliation and provider refunds. Persist per-source allocations and their original validity. At verification, capture only a still-valid reserved allocation; if it expires or a payment refund invalidates it, stop and obtain a new explicit authorization, never silently move it to another bucket. Cancellation releases a hold without creating a refund row for credits never debited. A settled reversal restores only the original eligible source under the approved rule; an ended free month cannot become purchased credit.

Transactional capture locks in the established request/profile/slot/wallet order where applicable, then the action and its grants in one documented order shared by all new paths. Review that order against current code rather than bolt on an action-first lock that can deadlock delivery. Final order is an implementation acceptance item. Within one DB transaction, recheck consent, policy, revision, reservation and verifier result, write the seeker debit using the keyed ledger primitive, record settlement and eligible credit reward obligation, and consume the reservation. Atomic failure rolls all effects back. Notification/outreach intent belongs in a transactional outbox; external sends never run while holding DB locks. Workers reconcile ambiguous delivery and settlement instead of retrying blindly.

Enforce unique economic-unit settlement, provider/method/receipt identity, action/evidence identity and effect-specific idempotency keys. Bind a key to a canonical payload fingerprint; reused keys with different quotes or recipients fail as conflicts. Two verifiers racing, evidence arriving after cancellation, duplicate callbacks, stale worker leases and a crash after commit must yield one economic result. Separate seeker debit, reward grant and reversal keys; a single key reused across different effects is not sufficient. Daily reconciliation compares reservations, captures, reward obligations and reversals by policy version and reports missing or duplicate links.

Fraud controls must combine canonical-person identity, current work-email verification, pair and campaign velocity, shared payment/device risk signals where lawful, duplicate evidence detection and reward caps. Self-referrals, linked aliases, reused receipt IDs and circular referral rings cannot earn. Repeated candidate/referrer/role combinations across agents share an economic unit. Detect coordinated reciprocal attestations and reused employers' receipt references; quarantine suspicious clusters without publicly accusing users. Work email proves mailbox access, not authority to recruit. Clicks, dwell time and tracking pixels never unlock a reward. Use appealable holds and minimal pseudonymous risk data, not indiscriminate contact or device harvesting.

Candidate packets are useful preparation, not permission to leak. The seeker confirms the facts and each recipient scope. Before acceptance, send only the existing anonymous role/skills preview; D-019's no name/resume before acceptance still holds. After acceptance, a short-lived recipient-bound document grant may expose the explicitly consented resume and job-specific briefing. Include relevant experience with source references, requirements matched or missing, questions and the seeker's confirmed note. Never invent credentials or let resume/job-page text issue agent instructions.

Consent names purpose, recipient or narrowly defined recipient scope, fields, expiry and revocation. No broad insider mailing list. Recheck bilateral blocks, verified employment, current grant and consent at send and read time, not just at packet generation. Revoke access on pass, withdrawal, block or expiration consistently with existing referralDocumentAccessGrants and review-link flows. Do not attach raw resumes to email. Keep packets behind authenticated scoped access; redact employer-confidential data, personal contacts and unrelated history. One recipient's link must not work for another.

Phase 1 uses synthetic fixtures only, no packet persistence or model calls. A later consented pilot must approve packet retention and evidence retention separately before storing any real material. Proposed minimum is deletion at the end of the authorized action's access window with a short, specified dispute hold only when required; exact windows and legal bases must be documented before launch. Retain only pseudonymous settlement/audit facts for the applicable financial requirement, with restricted access and deletion mapping. Provider no-training terms, subprocessors, geography, deletion and logging must be verified before sending candidate data. An immutable digest is not permission to retain raw PII forever.

Outreach is opt-in per destination with a visible sender, job-specific purpose, suppression list, per-recipient and global caps, and immediate pause/revoke. Never scrape personal inboxes or auto-enroll contacts. D-141 agent scopes, expiry, draft default and ask-every-time must remain effective. Agents cannot buy credits, expand their own authorization or exceed daily/weekly limits through multiple keys. Consent withdrawal blocks queued unsent work; a send already performed is explained truthfully. Late receipts cannot bypass revoked authorization or trigger another capture.
