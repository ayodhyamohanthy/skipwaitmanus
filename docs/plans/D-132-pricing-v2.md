1. **Flags off is today.** `/plans` renders the legacy page, every ask spends 1 credit, the quote answers 1, and no copy changes (an App-level test with the flags off).
2. **The level is stored once.** #415 classifies the job again at every spend. Store the level, and the cost it gives, once at ask creation with its source, the way `jobs.jobFunction` is stored (migration 0077 is the model; take the next free migration number from plan §5). 3. **Short of credits.** At send to a covered company: no ask, nothing spent, and the server returns its quote so the screen offers the one-tap top-up ("This ask costs 5 credits. You have 3.
