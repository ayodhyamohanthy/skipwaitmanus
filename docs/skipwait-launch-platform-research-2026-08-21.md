# SkipWait.me: Product-Launch Platform Research

## Scope

This is a targeted submission list, not an attempt to post indiscriminately to every directory on the internet. The selected platforms have a current direct submission route, a technology-product audience, and a reasonable fit for a public job-referral PWA. Every listing must use truthful copy, a working public URL, and no fabricated users, results, testimonials, activity, or hiring guarantees.

| Priority | Platform | Submission fit | Current requirement / constraint | Recommended status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) | Strong launch-day discovery fit | A personal account, direct product URL, name, tagline, topics, thumbnail, pricing, description, gallery, makers, and first comment are required or recommended. | 1 | [BetaList](https://betalist.com/submit) | Strong early-stage-startup fit | The product must be relatively new, a technology startup, not previously featured, offer meaningful landing-page information, and let visitors access or sign up. | Prepare draft; submit after verifying the canonical public launch URL. |

## Launch integrity rules

SkipWait’s public launch copy must state only what the product currently does: it helps Job Seekers submit private referral requests for identified employers and lets verified company-email Referrers review requests at no charge. It must never promise a job, interview, employer response, priority, access to hidden employees, or a hiring outcome.

The source deployment must remain the public launch target until VibecodeApp staging parity and controlled cutover evidence are complete. The canonical hostname should not be claimed as live on directories until it actually points to the verified source deployment or a later verified Vibecode deployment.
