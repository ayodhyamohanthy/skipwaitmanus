# D-100.authz-account: authorization and personal data on the account, messaging, employer, agent and public routes

Read-only audit (packet `docs/plans/D-100-security-audit.md`, Tier 3, rule 1 binds). Auditor: Hark · a2b5, 2026-10-30.
**`main` read:** `2d72dec7f1609b9e8a3b7e6ec6bc4062bb03aee0`. Line numbers below are on that commit.

**Files in scope:** `server/profileRoutes.ts`, `dmRoutes.ts`, `followRoutes.ts`, `employerRoutes.ts`, `companyClaimRoutes.ts`, `companyDirectoryRoutes.ts`, `jobLinkPreviewRoutes.ts` (with the fetcher in `jobLinkPreview.ts`), `jobCatalogRoutes.ts`, `impactCardRoutes.ts`, `passwordResetRoutes.ts`, `oauthRoutes.ts` (with `oauthStore.ts`), `seekerAgentRoutes.ts`, `skippieSeekerRoutes.ts`, `assistantRoutes.ts`, `mcpRoutes.ts`, `sitemapRoutes.ts` and `plansCatalogRoutes.ts`, their wiring in `server/_core/index.ts`, and the `server/db.ts` functions those routes call (read by line range only).

## Summary
- **Withheld under rule 1:** 1 finding (H-1). It is not described here. Its details are held for the CEO outside the repository. Its severity is not given here either, so this file says nothing about it.
- **Published:** 13 findings: 1 P2 and 12 P3. None has a path an attacker can use on their own. The P2 is a control that does less than its screen says, with no attacker path while password sign-in is not live.
- **Held up under reading:**
  - Every signed-in route takes the user id from the session (`resolveIdentity`), never from the request, and every read or write of an owned row filters by that id: profile, setup, work items (`profileRoutes.ts:43-206`), assistant connections, tokens, approvals and developer apps (`db.ts:560`, `:606`, `:656`, `:710`, `:806`, `:813`), seeker agent grants (`seekerAgentRoutes.ts:298-350`, `getGrantForUser`), and the impact card (`impactCardRoutes.ts:17-25`).
  - Every `/api/admin/*` route in scope checks `role === "admin"` before reading or writing: partners and sponsorships (`employerRoutes.ts:73-78`, `:242-297`), companies (`companyDirectoryRoutes.ts:63-72`, `:118-158`) and job sources (`jobCatalogRoutes.ts:161-175`).
  - Employer talent discovery is gated on an **approved** employer account (`db.ts:5285-5289`), shows only seekers who opted in with an active consent and only the consented fields, coarsened (`db.ts:5409-5413`), resolves talent through per-employer opaque references (`db.ts:5405`), and opens a snapshot only inside an unexpired, unrevoked unlock (`db.ts:5415`). The billing email must equal the verified sign-in email (`employerRoutes.ts:85-87`).
  - Assistant-proposed asks run only after the owner approves them, at the cost the owner saw (`assistantRoutes.ts:218-242`), through a conditional status update so a double approve cannot run twice (`db.ts:719-735`).
  - OAuth: public clients with PKCE S256 only, single-use hashed codes bound to client and redirect (`oauthStore.ts:43-60`), exact redirect match on authorize and on the consent lookup (`oauthRoutes.ts:66-81`), and https or loopback redirects only (`oauthStore.ts:10-17`). The consent screen shows the callback host (`client/src/pages/ConnectAssistant.tsx:45-46`, `:86`).
  - MCP and agent bearers are shape-checked before any lookup, stored as hashes, and refused when revoked, expired, paused or without entitlement (`mcpRoutes.ts:44-64`, `seekerAgentRoutes.ts:129-141`).
  - The job-link fetcher pins DNS, refuses any private, loopback, link-local, CGNAT or IPv4-mapped answer, follows redirects by hand with the same check on every hop, and caps time (5 s per hop) and size (500 KB) (`jobLinkPreview.ts:15-25`). Its route caps the body at 8 KB and the rate at 12 a minute per client IP (`jobLinkPreviewRoutes.ts:5`). `req.ip` cannot be spoofed while every request passes through the edge Worker (`_core/index.ts:134-144`).
  - The company claim routes are behind their flag before auth and every answer is `private, no-store` (`companyClaimRoutes.ts:15-19`); bodies are strict Zod (`:11-12`).
  - The follower and following lists return a bare count of "Member" labels, never ids or names (`followRoutes.ts:81`, `:93`). The public profile hides the name that would rebuild a verified work email and never returns the email (`db.ts:894-912`).
  - Seeker Skippy is behind its flag and a fixed allow-list of test accounts, and a chosen resume must belong to the caller (`skippieSeekerRoutes.ts:71-82`, `skippieSeeker.ts:275-278`).
  - DM sends need an `Idempotency-Key`, re-check suspension, the referrer gate and the follow rule inside the transaction, and keep a durable 30-an-hour quota (`db.ts:3775`).

## Notes for the CEO
- No route in scope returns another person's email, phone, resume or work-email address. The employer snapshot is the only cross-account personal data, and it is consent-gated, field-limited and time-limited.
- `companyDirectoryRoutes.ts` and `plansCatalogRoutes.ts` are public by design: they serve only the company directory, employee-posted roles with an anonymous or first-name poster label (`shared/companyOpportunities.ts:45-48`), and the plan catalog.
- `employerRoutes.ts` takes the unlock-credit purchase only through a durable local checkout intent with an `Idempotency-Key` (`employerRoutes.ts:115-122`). The money side of that path belongs to `D-100.authz-admin-money` and was not audited here.
