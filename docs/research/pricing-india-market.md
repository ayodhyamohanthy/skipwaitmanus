# India job-seeker pricing: comparables and takeaways for SkipWait

**Bottom line:** In India, people pay about **₹999 for a month** of a job-seeker product, or **₹2,000–₹3,000 for 3–6 months**. Above that, the next things people buy are human help sold per session (₹99–₹1,500) and bootcamps that cost lakhs. The current INR draft prices Momentum (₹1,699) above every Indian job platform, and it prices Land as monthly subscriptions. That does not work well with UPI AutoPay limits, and Indian buyers mostly pay one time for a fixed period.

All pages were seen on **2026-10-14** unless a different date is given. "App Store IN" means the in-app purchase list on Apple's India App Store, which gives prices but not durations.

## B. How Indians pay, and GST

- **UPI is most of the volume.** It carried 85.5% of payment transactions in the second half of 2025 ([FPJ, citing RBI](https://www.freepressjournal.in/tech/upi-commands-largest-share-of-transaction-volume-at-855-in-2025-2nd-half-rbi-report)).
- **UPI AutoPay is growing fast.** It went from 58M transactions in January 2024 to 175M in January 2025 ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/personal-finance/upi-autopay-growth-2025-overtakes-cards-payments-npci-125022000316_1.html); the page returned 403, so this figure comes from the search index).
- **One-time payment habit.** A payments vendor describes India as "historically… a one-time-payment market" ([FastSpring, 2026-05-14](https://fastspring.com/blog/introducing-upi-autopay/)). That is a vendor's view. The pattern above supports it: Indian job tools sell fixed periods of 1, 3 or 6 months, and Naukri explicitly sells "one-time, no renewal".
- **Limits on automatic renewals:**
  - Automatic charges up to **₹15,000** need no extra authentication; anything above needs approval each time ([Khaitan, on RBI's June 2022 circular](https://www.khaitanco.com/thought-leaderships/RBI-enhances-transaction-limits-for-processing-of-e-mandates-for-recurring-transactions:-from-INR-5000-to-INR-15000)).
  - The customer must be notified 24 hours before each charge ([Axis Bank](https://axisbank.com/retail/cards/recurring-payments)).
  - Chargebee's Razorpay page says the default maximum for a UPI mandate is **₹5,000** and that you contact support to raise it. The same page also lists ₹15,000 ([Chargebee docs](https://www.chargebee.com/docs/payments/2.0/payment-gateways-and-configuration/razorpay-upi-netbank)), so the cap set on our account needs confirming.
  - PhonePe and BHIM do not support mandates above ₹15,000 ([Chargebee KB](https://www.chargebee.com/docs/payments/1.0/kb/billing/razorpay-upi-autopay-supported-applications-and-their-handles)).
  - A single UPI payment to a business stays capped at **₹1 lakh** except in categories NPCI has notified ([newsonair, Sept 2025](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/npci-raises-upi-person-to-merchant-transaction-limit-to-%E2%82%B95-lakh)).
- **GST:**
  - The rate on online services is 18% ([LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1456938)). I could not confirm in the official notification that this rate survived the GST rate overhaul of 22 September 2025.
  - A foreign seller of online services must register for GST from its very first sale to an Indian consumer ([India Briefing](https://www.india-briefing.com/news/oidar-compliance-india-gst-registration-ntor-rules-gstr5a-digital-tax-43951.html/)).
- **Consumer law:** India's 2023 guidelines on deceptive design ban two relevant practices ([SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2023/12/04/ccpa-notifies-guidelines-for-prevention-and-regulation-of-dark-patterns-2023-legal-news/)):
  - "Drip pricing": not showing the full price up front.
  - "Subscription trap": including requiring payment details or auto-debit approval to get a free plan.
  - The regulator fined FirstCry ₹2 lakh for adding GST at checkout ([Medianama, 2025-09-29](https://www.medianama.com/2025/09/223-ccpas-rs-2-lakh-fine-firstcry-e-commerce-discount/)).
- **The grey market:**
  - Scammers advertise "referrals" to firms like Deloitte and McKinsey, then sell a ₹1,000 "resume review" paid by UPI, which leaves little trace ([Decode, updated 2025-10-06](https://www.decodeinternet.in/decode/linkedin-job-scams-fake-resume-reviews-upskilling-trap-job-seekers-fake-job-offers-recruitment-fraud-26455)).
  - Employer referral policies penalize "unethical practices" by the person referring ([greytHR template](https://www.greythr.com/downloadable-resource/employee-referral-policy)).
  - I could not find indexed Telegram or WhatsApp price lists for paid referrals; the one public channel I checked charged nothing. Topmate is the visible paid market.
  - Why it is risky for a seeker: the scam pattern above; the employee who refers can be disciplined under their employer's policy; résumés get passed around in groups; and a UPI payment to an individual is hard to recover.

## C. Takeaways for pricing SkipWait in India

The price levels below are my recommendations, not findings.

1. **Momentum is priced above the market.** Every Indian job-platform Pro plan costs ₹999 a month (Naukri Pro, Unstop Pro); only LinkedIn charges ₹1,850–₹1,899. ₹1,699 sits above all of them. Suggest **Momentum ₹999 and Start ₹499**. Start could stay at ₹699, but ₹499/₹999 gives a clean 2× step. After GST, ₹999 brings in ₹847 (about $9.85), roughly 49% of the $20 price — similar to LinkedIn's India discount of about 55% against its USD price.
2. **Replace yearly INR plans with 3- and 6-month passes.** Indian comparables are much more generous for longer terms:
   - Naukri charges 2× the monthly price for 3 months and 3× for 6.
   - Unstop charges 6× for a year.
   - SkipWait's yearly price is 10× the monthly price, the smallest discount in the set, and a 12-month term is longer than most job hunts.
   - Suggested: Momentum ₹2,499 for 3 months and ₹3,999 for 6; Start ₹1,199 for 3 months.
3. **Land in India should be prepaid passes, not monthly renewals.**
   - Sprint (₹16,999) and Concierge (₹42,999) are above the ₹15,000 limit for automatic charges, so every renewal would need the buyer's approval. Land Focus (₹8,499) and the yearly Start plan (₹6,990) are above Chargebee's default ₹5,000 mandate cap.
   - Concierge yearly (₹4.3 lakh) and Sprint yearly (₹1.7 lakh) are above the ₹1 lakh UPI cap and could only be paid by card.
   - Sell Land as 30- or 90-day passes paid once by UPI or card. Naukri's "one-time price, no renewal" is the local precedent.
4. **Do not undercut the power users who will pay.** Keep Land Focus near **₹8,499 a month (for example, ₹19,999 for 90 days)**. Scaler's EMI of ₹9,791 a month shows a serious job-hunt budget of ₹8,000–₹10,000 a month exists. Concierge could be application-only, for example from ₹39,999 for 90 days.
5. **Give explorers something under ₹200 that needs no subscription.**
   - At about ₹86 a credit, the 5-credit pack (₹429) costs more than a median human résumé review on Topmate (₹410).
   - Add one-time UPI packs: **₹99 for 2 credits (or one extra open ask for 30 days)** and ₹199 for 5.
   - Price the larger packs at about ₹45–₹55 a credit.
6. **Keep the free plan generous and never ask for a card.** Three open asks already beats Naukri's free tier (3 AI attempts in total) and Unstop's (3 credits a year). Requiring auto-debit approval for a free plan counts as a "subscription trap" under the 2023 guidelines.
7. **Show every ₹ price including GST ("incl. GST"), not the way LinkedIn does it.** After the FirstCry penalty, adding GST at checkout is a legal risk. If prices include GST, the revenue SkipWait keeps is: ₹699 → ₹592, ₹999 → ₹847, ₹8,499 → ₹7,203. Check with a CA whether SkipWait sells from an Indian entity (regular GST) or a foreign one (GST registration from the first sale), and set up Chargebee's taxes to match.
8. **Use ₹x99 prices and honest anchors.** Indian sellers cluster at ₹99, ₹499, ₹999, ₹1,999, ₹2,999 and ₹5,999, and lean on struck-through prices (Unstop, Internshala "65% OFF… valid till 15th Oct", Naukri's 25% off). SkipWait's rules ban fake "was" prices. Honest alternatives:
   - per-day framing (₹999 is "₹33 a day"; iimjobs used "under ₹15 a day");
   - the real saving on a 3-month pass;
   - "a paid referral call elsewhere costs ₹200–₹1,500; referrals here are free."
9. **Say plainly what paying does not buy.** Indian buyers are used to paying for priority: Naukri's Featured Profile (₹890) and Priority Applicant (₹971), Early Applicant on iimjobs and hirist (₹1,499), and iimjobs' old recruiter nudges. SkipWait should never call anything "boost", "priority" or "featured". Paid plans should promise only more open asks, more finished work and human help.
10. **Use the grey market as the trust story and the way in.** Topmate already sells "referrals" for ₹5–₹1,500, and one listing is $500. Scammers charge ₹1,000 by UPI. SkipWait's free referrals from verified employees are the safe alternative. Giving away referral-ask templates for free would also work: Topmate sells them for ₹10–₹49 with up to 992 ratings.
11. **Price human work to Indian rates.** On Topmate, a mock interview has a median of ₹809 and a résumé review ₹410; InterviewBuddy charges ₹1,499 for an expert. SkipWait's human expert review (25 credits) comes to about ₹2,150 at current pack prices. Either price it near ₹750–₹1,000 in India or present it as review by a senior person at the target company.
12. **Set up payments by rail.** Use one-time UPI for small packs and passes, and UPI AutoPay only for Start and Momentum monthly, as long as they stay under the mandate cap set on our Chargebee account. Use cards for anything over ₹1 lakh. Keep USD through PayPal for Indians abroad and for anyone targeting jobs overseas.
