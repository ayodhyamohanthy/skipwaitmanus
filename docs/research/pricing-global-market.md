## 2. Takeaways for global USD pricing

1. **Most monthly plans cost $29–$40; SkipWait's Start and Momentum are below all of them.** Teal $29, Rezi $29, Remote Rocketship $29, Simplify $39.99, LinkedIn $39.99 (3P), Jobright $39.99 (3P), Huntr $40. The ends of the range are Careerflow $23.99, Kickresume $24 and Jobscan $49.95.
   - **Start $8** is the cheapest paid plan in the set. Keep it as the entry plan for people who are just exploring.
   - **Momentum at $20** is under-priced next to tools that sell less than SkipWait does. Testing **$24–$29** would still sit at or below every tool except Careerflow and Kickresume.

2. **Quarterly is the main plan for an active job search, and SkipWait doesn't have one.** Five tools land on almost the same price: Teal $79, Jobscan $89.95, Simplify $89.99, Jobright $89.99 (3P), Huntr $90.
   - That works out to about $26–$30 a month for a 3-month search.
   - Jobscan and Simplify both label quarterly "Most popular".
   - Jobscan offers its free trial on quarterly only.
   - Typical quarterly discount: 25% (Simplify, Huntr, Kickresume, Careerflow). Range is 9% (Teal) to 40% (Jobscan).
   - **Suggestion:** a Momentum 3-month plan at about **$49** (vs $60 at monthly rates, about 18% off). A Land quarterly plan could follow the same pattern.

3. **Annual plans mostly don't exist for search-only tools.**
   - No annual plan at Teal, Simplify, Jobright or Jobscan. Huntr's longest plan is 6 months.
   - Annual plans appear only for products used across a career: LinkedIn ($239.88, 50% off), Careerflow (40% off), Kickresume (67%), Remote Rocketship (72%), Aced (85%).
   - SkipWait's yearly plan (10× monthly, 17% off) is honest. A quarterly plan will earn more than a deeper annual discount.

4. **Weekly passes are sold to people who are exploring or in a hurry, at about 1.6–2.2× the monthly rate.** Weekly × 52/12 against the monthly price: Teal 1.94×, Jobright 1.95×, Simplify 2.17×, Careerflow 1.62×.
   - These passes renew automatically and draw complaints; reviewers warn that Simplify's $19.99 weekly adds up fast.
   - SkipWait's **5 credits for $5** pack is already the cheapest way in of anything here.
   - **Suggestion:** sell it as a **non-renewing 7-day pass** (about $5) rather than a weekly subscription. It keeps exploring users without the renewal trap.

5. **Free tiers give away the core product and meter AI with small quotas that reset.**
   - Jobscan: 5 scans a month. Refer Me: 1 of each AI credit a month. Jobright: credits reset daily. Aced: 5 peer mocks a month. Teal: 10 bullets + 2 summaries + 2 cover letters.
   - SkipWait's free credits are **3, one time only**. **Suggestion:** a small monthly allowance (1–3 credits) gives exploring users a reason to come back.
   - SkipWait's **3 free open asks** are more generous than Refer Me, whose free tier includes **no** referral requests.

6. **Competitors already sell referral access and priority. This is SkipWait's main difference and its red line.**
   - Aced Pro ($12–$79/mo) puts paying members "at the top of the list" for 400+ referrers.
   - Refer Me sells 40 requests a month ($149/yr, about $0.31 each), "prioritized request matching", paid profile boosts, and refills at $39 per 40 (about $0.98 each).
   - FindMyReferral has seekers pay referrers ₹1,299–₹2,499 per referral.
   - Refer takes 20% of the first month's pay (3P).
   - This proves people will pay. SkipWait's line should be "referral access is free and can't be bought."
   - **Watch:** the open-ask caps (3/8/15/30/50/unlimited) and the "extra slot for 2 credits" sell *volume*, which is close to what Refer Me charges for. Copy and the referrer-side UI should never show a seeker's plan.

7. **People who want a referral "at any cost" pay far more than $500.**
   - Find My Profession: $1,500–$3,000 a month, which is $12k–$14k over a 4-month search at a $100k salary.
   - interviewing.io dedicated coaching: about $1.5k–$2k for 3 sessions (3P).
   - Also: Aced coaching $249/hour (3P), Scale.jobs up to $1,099, TopResume Executive $599.
   - **Land Concierge at $500/mo is at the bottom of the human-service range.** **Suggestion:** keep $100/$200/$500, and add "Concierge from $1,000/mo, by application", priced by human hours.
   - The paid work must stay human labour: prep, research, writing asks, managing applications. Never referrer access or priority.
   - Success fees (Find My Profession 8%, Refer 20% of month one) do exist. Treat them with caution under SkipWait's "no hiring guarantees" rule.

8. **SkipWait's human-review credit is priced far below the market.**
   - Market prices for human work: TopResume $179–$599, Aced $249/hour (3P), interviewing.io $179–$339 per session (3P). Rezi bundles 1 expert review a month into its $29 Pro plan.
   - SkipWait's **"Human expert review = 25 credits ($25)"** is about 7–14× cheaper than TopResume.
   - **Suggestion:** either define it as a narrow written review (around 15 minutes of reviewer time) or reprice it to about **49–99 credits**.
   - The AI mock interview at 5 credits looks fine next to Aced Expert ($20/mo, billed annually).

9. **Applying in bulk is cheap, so SkipWait shouldn't compete on volume.**
   - Bots: JobCopilot about $28–$32/mo, LazyApply $99–$999 a year.
   - Simplify+ includes 20 auto-applies a week.
   - Humans applying: Scale.jobs $0.40–$1.00 per application, Careerify about $3.
   - SkipWait's credits should be framed as **quality per ask**, not number of applications.

10. **Avoid competitors' anchoring tricks (SkipWait's pricing ethics already rule them out).**
    - Final Round shows $150 monthly next to $25/mo yearly, a decoy that makes yearly look 83% off.
    - Careerflow shows a crossed-out "$45" (or "₹1499" in India) on every plan.
    - Kickresume runs a "limited-time 20%" discount (seen in the India view).
    - Gifting is a pattern worth copying: Kickresume puts **$72 / $120 of friend vouchers** in its quarterly and yearly plans. SkipWait could add "gift N credits" to quarterly and yearly plans, through the existing invite-reward system.

11. **Trials and refunds are where tools compete on trust.**
    - Jobscan: 7-day trial, quarterly plan only. Refer Me: 7-day trial. Teal: no card needed to start.
    - Refunds: Rezi 30 days, Kickresume and Remote Rocketship 14 days, Aced 5 days.
    - Final Round's monthly plan can't be refunded. Huntr and Simplify have no trial.
    - **Suggestion:** a 7-day money-back on a customer's first paid purchase, set up in Chargebee. It costs little and lowers the risk for exploring users.

**What the regional evidence says:**
- **USD everywhere is the norm** for US job-search tools.
- **Local currency at roughly exchange-rate parity** appears only for GBP and CAD (Remote Rocketship), plus LinkedIn's full local pricing (EUR, and AUD per one source). Remote Rocketship rounds to friendly numbers: £21 and CA$39 for $29.
- **UK human services are priced lower than in the US**: TopCV £99 vs TopResume $179, about 25% less after conversion at roughly 1.33 $/£ (approximate rate, not verified).
- **Bigger discounts based on local buying power show up only in India** (Careerflow, Kickresume). Measured that way, SkipWait's straight ₹86/$ INR draft is 2–3× more expensive than what those competitors charge in India.

**Suggestion for SkipWait:**
- Bill USD globally.
- Add GBP, EUR, CAD and AUD at exchange-rate parity, rounded down to friendly numbers, with VAT-inclusive display in the UK and EU. Examples: Start ≈ £6 / €7 / CA$11 / A$12. These use approximate exchange rates; recompute them at launch.
- Keep **USD in the UAE and Singapore**, since no competitor prices locally there. The AED is pegged at 3.6725 per dollar, so Start would be about AED 29 if you ever localize.
- Use discounts based on local buying power only for India.
