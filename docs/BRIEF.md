## What SkipWait is
- skipwait.me: a job seeker sends one ask (an official job link from the company's careers site or ATS, a resume and a short note); only employees of that employer who verified their work email can see it, accept it and refer.
- Goal (D-010, D-026): the go-to global site for job referrals, getting people referred faster. Compass: referrals are the product; credits are the revenue engine (1 credit = 1 ask); quality is the moat; never risk anyone's money.

## Rules you must not break
- **Frozen to Nov 17 (D-059; change only for a P0 or new data):** D-004, D-005, D-006, D-010, D-012(3), D-019, D-023, D-026, D-030 with D-049/D-057, D-031, D-039, D-041, D-042, D-044, D-055.
- **Login (D-030, D-049, D-057).** Seekers: Google, Microsoft, GitHub, LinkedIn or Slack, or personal email + password, on skipwait.me, never an authkit.app page; no six-digit code: sign-up and an unconfirmed address get an emailed link that sets the password, and a password opens a session only after that link (D-078). Referrers: a work-email code only, on every visit and every referrer surface; their work email never gets a password or a social session, and neither does any address at a company with a verified referrer (D-081); use `referrerSignInHref()` (`client/src/lib/referrerSignIn.ts`), never `SignInButton` or `openSignIn` there. Pinned by `client/src/pages/loginRule.test.tsx` and `signIn.test.tsx`.
- **Identity.** One canonical person plus verified login aliases: match the exact alias first, never link or merge on an unverified email, never combine two people's credits, asks or referrals, and a suspension or sign-out covers every alias (AGENTS.md identity rules).
- **Money.**
  - Never choose an option that spends the owner's money (D-031). Referrers are never paid or charged: no credits, perks or purchases for referring (D-055).
  - Fairness (D-006, D-040): never sell referrals, a ranking of people, priority, visibility or seeker-to-referrer payments; employers buy only labeled job ads and tooling; referrers never see a seeker's plan; at most 2 open asks per company.
  - A money path that cannot deliver is switched off, never deleted without the owner's say-so (D-042; D-067 is that say-so for Pro/Max), and nothing is offered that the code does not grant (D-044).
  - Credits today: an ask to a company with an available referrer spends 1 credit at send (no credit, no ask). An ask to a company with none waits for free up to 90 days (D-034, D-061) and spends its credit when a referrer can first see it; with no credit then it is held, never delivered free or billed later (D-047). The credit comes back once on a withdrawal before pickup or an expiry 7 days after first visibility with nobody picking it up, unless its month or plan has ended; after a pickup it stays spent, even if that referrer passes (D-021, D-050). Not built yet: the day-30 reconfirm (with D-060) and the pass reason deciding the credit (D-035, D-066). Referrer timers (D-038) are only partly on `main` and do not run yet. Open questions: D-076.
  - Say it truthfully: "3 asks free every month; more use credits. Referrers are never paid and never ask you for money." (D-063, `shared/moneyCopy.ts`). Never call referrals simply "free".
  - Catalog (D-036, D-039, D-041, D-046): Free gets 3 credits a month (after a card check once the owner switches D-079 on; off today), no welcome credits, no reverse trial (D-051); credit packs, 30/90-day passes, Keep warm yearly and Concierge by application go on sale from `/plans` once the owner applies the D-069 Chargebee sync (live) and GO-LIVE-1 ships (D-067 is live: Pro/Max are gone and referrers cannot buy).   - Seeker payments run only through Chargebee (Razorpay for INR, PayPal for USD; D-004). Never make a real payment (the owner runs the one live test, D-065); use provider test modes, mocks and fixtures. Read `docs/PAYMENTS_TEST_RUNBOOK.md` before touching payments, refunds or credits.
- **Privacy and trust.** A referrer sees no seeker name or resume, and no seeker can message a verified referrer, until that referrer accepts (D-019(e), D-012(3)). A block hides two people from each other everywhere, including delivery. No public per-referrer metrics, invented numbers, sample data, dark patterns, false urgency or pressure on referrers. Terms and Privacy keep live legal text; agents never reword Terms, Privacy, Refund or Cancellation; they list the lines for the owner in plan §6.
- **Basics always work (D-019).** Payments, logins and the seeker, referrer and admin flows. A broken basic is a P0: restore it (no approval needed), verify it live, report. Changing how a basic works needs the owner.
- **No half-baked flows.** Ship a flow end to end or not at all: every failure is logged, alerted and shown in plain words; every screen handles loading, empty, error and offline; every money change is idempotent, reconciled and recoverable. A user with a pending action gets one idempotent email with a direct link, at most one a day (D-060).
- **Design (D-048, D-070, D-071).** `DESIGN.md` is the goal-UI contract (phase 2 shipped: tokens in `client/src/index.css`, primitives in `client/src/components/goal/`); approved comps `design/goal-ui/pick/`. Until a screen is moved over, keep its tokens and components; never re-skin ad hoc.
- **The repo is public (D-059).** Never commit a secret or put one in a `VITE_*` key; no business numbers or personal data in docs, fixtures or commits.

## How to ship
- Stack: Express, React, TypeScript, Azure MySQL with Drizzle, Tailwind, React Query; Cloudflare Containers (API) and Pages (web); pnpm 10, Node 22+.
- Branch or worktree per lane; never force-push, rewrite history, `git reset --hard` a shared tree or commit another session's files. Failing test first for logic. Zod at every boundary, shared contracts in `shared/`, no `any`. MySQL has no row-level security: every query checks the signed-in owner and company scope, with a cross-user test. Compute times in the app, never compare MySQL `NOW()` with `Date.now()`. Logs and Sentry never carry tokens, query strings, personal data or single-use links. No new dependency without the owner. Declare env keys in `.env.example`; server secrets need the `src/worker.ts` passthrough.
- Gate, on your commit merged with the latest `origin/main`: `pnpm check`; `node scripts/design-token-audit.mjs`; `bash scripts/lint-deploy-migrations.sh`; `node scripts/check-deploy-triggers.mjs`; `LANG=en_US.UTF-8 pnpm vitest run`; `pnpm build`.
- **Green before `main` (D-082).** Push only while `main`'s latest CI is green (while it is red, push nothing but the fix, and tell the owner), after the gate passes, and once GitHub CI is green on that exact commit: push your branch, open a pull request (draft for Tier 1), wait for every check, then merge it or fast-forward `main` to that commit. Never land with a check failing, skipped or still running; never weaken a test or use `--no-verify`.
- A push to `main` deploys what it touches (D-003). Watch CI and both deploys to the end; if anything goes red, revert or fix your commit first. Then `EXPECTED_SHA=<sha> bash scripts/verify-live-release.sh` must print CONVERGED for API changes, skipwait.me must serve `<meta name="git-commit">` with your SHA for web changes, and affected screens get checked at 1280 and 390. Mark the row done with the SHA.
- Migrations: take the next free number after `drizzle/deploy/` and the numbers claimed in plan §5; never destructive; schema-dependent code lands only after its migration is verified.
- Owner actions waiting: plan §6. System map: `AGENTS.md`, "Living system map".
