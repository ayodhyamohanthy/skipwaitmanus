# .impeccable.md — skipwait.me design context

> Read this before any design work on this project. It is the source of truth for
> brand voice, palette, type, and the principles that arbitrate design decisions.

## Design Context

### Users

**Job Seekers (primary).** Early-to-mid career, applying to companies where they
have no connection. They are on a phone, often late at night, and quietly anxious
about asking a stranger for help. Job to be done: get one real human at the target
company to actually look at their application — without it feeling like begging or
networking theatre.

**Verified Referrers (secondary).** Employees who opted in to help. They are at
work, on a break, skimming. Job to be done: decide quickly and privately whether to
help this person — without their employer, or the candidate, learning who they are
until they choose to reveal it.

**Employers (tertiary).** Pay for sponsored roles and talent unlocks. They are
buying attention, not a relationship.

### Brand Personality

**Quietly confident.** Calm, credible, understated. It behaves like a well-connected
friend who happens to work there: it never oversells, never gamifies, never
pressures.

The emotional goal is **relief**. The user should feel that the awkward part has
already been handled for them.

**Voice:** plain, specific, warm but not chummy. Short sentences. No exclamation
marks, no hype words, no emoji. Say the true thing rather than the exciting thing —
"Only you can see this" beats "Your privacy is our priority!"

### Aesthetic Direction

**Warm editorial.** The app's original terracotta/cream/brown identity — which is
still present in the markup — becomes the real brand. Deliberately un-Google and
un-SaaS.

- **Palette:** terracotta primary on warm paper; deep warm-brown ink; a deep pine
  green as the cool counterpoint for trust and verified states.
- **Type:** `Fraunces` (variable serif) for display — warm, slightly characterful,
  editorial. `Instrument Sans` for UI and body. The existing `--font-serif` token
  becomes real instead of aliasing the sans.
- **Theme:** light + dark, both driven by the same tokens so they cannot drift.
- **Anti-reference — a consumer social app.** No swipe gestures, no streaks, no
  emoji reactions, no gamified counters, no celebratory confetti.
- **Also avoid:** generic AI-SaaS gradients and glassmorphism; anything that reads
  as a Google/Material product; the dense Indeed/LinkedIn job-board list.

### Design Principles

1. **One identity, one source.** Every colour resolves to a token. No palette may be
   layered over another with `!important` — that is exactly how this project ended
   up with two brands at once.
2. **Calm over clever.** Motion and decoration earn their place or they go. Nothing
   pulses, bounces, or celebrates.
3. **Privacy is the visual language.** Hidden identities and private states should
   *look* private — muted, sealed, quiet — not like locked treasure.
4. **Warm neutrals, always.** Never mix slate and stone. Every grey derives from the
   warm ink/paper pair.
5. **The ask is the product.** The referral request is the moment that matters; every
   other screen exists to make that one moment feel safe.
