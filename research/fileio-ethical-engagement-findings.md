# file.io inspiration audit for skipwait.me

The public file.io landing page centers one obvious action, makes the handoff outcome immediately understandable, and explains privacy in plain language. For skipwait.me, the relevant product lessons are a single next action per screen, fast visible confirmation after a meaningful step, a shareable artifact that is useful to the recipient, and clear privacy constraints.

The redesign should not copy file.io’s visual brand or introduce compulsive retention mechanisms. The appropriate adaptation is an ethical utility loop: identify the employer after a valid link, let the Job Seeker move forward immediately, show private request progress only when it changes, and offer voluntary, recipient-benefiting sharing after a useful result.

The engagement standard for this product remains: no fake activity, no forced sharing, no surprise rewards, no deceptive urgency, and no referral reward before server-side eligibility checks pass.
