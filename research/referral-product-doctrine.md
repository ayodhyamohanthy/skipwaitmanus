# skipwait.me Referral Product Doctrine

## The category we are building

skipwait.me is not a job board, professional social network, recruiter marketplace, or career-content feed. It is the **trusted action layer for job referrals**.

> **When someone needs to ask for a referral or give one, skipwait.me should be the obvious place to go.**

The desired mental shortcut is simple:

| Need | Default product |
|---|---|
| Search the web | Google |
| Find a video | YouTube |
| Send a message | WhatsApp |
| Ask for or give a job referral | **skipwait.me** |

## One promise

**A Job Seeker can make a respectful referral request in minutes. A verified employee can safely decide whether to help.**

The product has only two primary verbs:

| Role | Primary verb | Definition of done |
|---|---|---|
| Job Seeker | **Ask** | A complete referral packet reaches the right private company corridor. |
| Referrer | **Help** | A verified employee can review context and choose, without reputational or privacy pressure. |

Everything else must improve one of these verbs: reduce time, raise trust, increase request quality, improve matching, or make an appropriate return visit useful.

## Non-goals

skipwait.me should not compete on endless job listings, public profiles, creator content, feeds, networking vanity metrics, or social engagement time. Those features would make the product noisier and weaken its memorability.

The Opportunity Wall is permitted only as a **referral discovery entry point**. Admin tooling is permitted only as a **trust and marketplace-health steward**. Account settings, tokens, notifications, and AI are permitted only when they shorten or safeguard asking and helping.

## Product decisions test

Before adding a feature, ask five questions.

| Question | Required answer |
|---|---|
| Does it make asking for a referral faster, safer, or clearer? | Yes, or reject it. |
| Does it make giving a referral safer, easier, or more thoughtful? | Yes, or reject it. |
| Can it be explained in one sentence to a first-time user? | Yes, or simplify it. |
| Does it create real value without exposing employee identity or candidate documents? | Yes, or redesign it. |
| Would a user naturally tell one relevant person about it after success? | Yes, or improve the underlying value before adding sharing. |

## The right growth loop

The product loop is not “get more engagement.” It is:

1. A Job Seeker submits one high-quality, private ask.
2. A verified employee can review it with enough context to make a safe decision.
3. A helpful decision produces a clear, respectful outcome.
4. At that moment, either participant may optionally invite **one relevant person** at the same company or share one genuine hiring signal.
5. Private company coverage becomes stronger, so the next request has a better chance of reaching a willing Referrer.

This produces word of mouth because the recommendation is useful: “Use skipwait when you need a referral,” not “join another professional network.”

## Immediate product focus

The next three screens should embody the doctrine:

| Priority | Screen | Singular job to be done |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **My Requests** | Let a Job Seeker see the factual status of every active referral ask and act on the next appropriate step. |
| 2 | **My Company Inbox** | Let a verified Referrer see only meaningful, private requests that need a decision. |
| 3 | **Admin Flow Health** | Let the operator strengthen company coverage and remove friction without surveilling people. |

Success is not time spent. Success is a user completing an ask or a helpful decision with confidence—and recommending skipwait.me when a relevant person needs the same outcome.
