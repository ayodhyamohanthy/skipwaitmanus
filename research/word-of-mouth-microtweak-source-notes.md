# Word-of-Mouth Micro-Tweak Source Notes

## Source review — 18 August 2026

### Yale Insights: *How Should Companies Fuel Word of Mouth?*

URL: https://insights.som.yale.edu/insights/how-should-companies-fuel-word-of-mouth

The article summarizes research by Aniko Öry and Yuichiro Kamada. It frames word of mouth as a user decision with relationship and opportunity costs, rather than a mechanically available distribution channel. It reports that free access can reduce the cost of trying a product with positive network effects, while referral incentives can be useful when the economics and audience fit. It also cautions that the effective configuration depends on the product’s social properties, audience, and value structure.

**Application to skipwait.me:** retain the free monthly request allowance as the zero-risk trial, but never make sharing a condition of receiving core value. Sharing should happen only after an authentic value event and must offer a recipient a concrete benefit—such as a private company inbox, a useful verified opening, or a reusable referral asset. Avoid indiscriminate cash or token-for-invite mechanics while early marketplace trust and quality are being established.

### Harvard Business School: *Why Prosocial Referral Incentives Work*

URL: https://www.hbs.edu/faculty/Pages/item.aspx?num=56492

The publication abstract reports two field experiments and an incentive-compatible laboratory experiment in which recipient-benefiting referral offers recruited more new customers than sender-benefiting offers. It attributes the result to reputational benefits at the sharing stage and a direct reduction of the recipient’s action cost at the uptake stage.

**Application to skipwait.me:** any invitation must lead with the recipient’s utility—not the sender’s token balance, status, or platform-growth goal. A trusted colleague should be offered a private, work-email-verified inbox, a useful verified opening, or an easier way to help—not a vague request to “join.” No monetary or token reward should be introduced for simply sending invitations until quality and abuse controls are proven.

### Gallup: *The Lasting Impact of Exceptional Candidate Experiences*

URL: https://www.gallup.com/workplace/651650/lasting-impact-exceptional-candidate-experiences.aspx

Gallup reports that candidate experience shapes hiring perceptions and highlights accurate expectations, clear role information, efficiency, and respectful use of candidates’ time. It also notes that the time between applying and an offer materially affects candidate perceptions.

**Application to skipwait.me:** the strongest activation and paid-conversion micro-tweak is not urgency or artificial scarcity. It is plain status, accurate routing expectations, and a useful next action. Payment should appear after the user has exhausted a real allowance or needs continued access, never while they are uncertain whether a request was received.
