# skipwait.me Cold-Start Launch System

## The real starting point

The current aggregate marketplace state is **zero verified Referrers**, **zero active employee-posted opportunities**, and **six private referral requests**. That is not a reason to simulate activity. It is a reason to turn every authentic Job Seeker request into a precise, privacy-preserving supply-acquisition opportunity.

> **Operating rule:** A user must never be charged, shown a fabricated match, or encouraged to broadcast contacts simply because a company corridor has not yet reached coverage.

## What now happens in-product

| Moment | Job Seeker experience | Referrer experience | Trust rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| An employer already has verified coverage | The request is routed privately and consumes one valid credit. | Exact-domain verified employees can review it. | Only the resolved employer domain is eligible. |
| An employer has no verified coverage | The request is retained privately, **no credit is used**, and one calm next step appears. | No one is notified until a real matching employee verifies. | No empty-match theatre and no hidden charge. |
| The Job Seeker chooses to help establish coverage | They may share one privacy-safe invitation with a trusted employee. The request, resume, identity, and role details are omitted. | A matching employee sees a company-specific, passwordless work-email verification entry. | Participation and decision remain voluntary. |
| The first matching employee verifies | The waiting request becomes available in their private inbox. | They can claim, decline, or leave it untouched. | No automatic referral or social obligation. |

## The launch unit: a company–role corridor

Do not launch skipwait.me as a generic global directory. Launch one corridor where a founder or trusted community has legitimate access, such as a small set of product and engineering companies in one alumni network. The objective is **one complete, respectful referral interaction**, not a sign-up target.

The corridor is ready to expand only when each of the following is true:

| Signal | What to observe | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Verified coverage | Real work-email-verified Referrers in the chosen companies | Demand without supply produces broken promises. |
| First-response reliability | Requests receive a clear decision or status | “Skip wait” must mean reduced uncertainty, not a queue. |
| Referrer repeat use | A Referrer returns to review or share an opening voluntarily | Reuse validates that helping remains low-pressure. |
| Safe activation | Company invitations create matching verification without privacy complaints | The growth loop is useful, not coercive. |

## The first 30 days

Recruit supply before paid Job Seeker acquisition. Start with one-to-one outreach to genuine company or alumni anchors, using the existing company invitation route. Give each early Referrer a clear promise: their work email and identity remain private, they control whether to help, and they may post an internally shared opening even if no request exists yet. Do not reward invite volume, scrape contacts, or publish unverified openings.

Each week, review the coverage queue by employer domain. A waiting request is not a failure; it is a high-intent signal that tells the team where to recruit the next legitimate employee anchor. Expand only after one corridor produces useful matches and reliable response behavior.

## Actions deliberately excluded

skipwait.me will not use fake job listings, fake Referrers, fake testimonials, bulk contact imports, automatic invitations, public employee directories, referral guarantees, or credit deductions for uncovered companies. These tactics may inflate a superficial number but would destroy the privacy and reputation protection that make Referrers willing to participate.

## Evidence basis

Marketplace guidance emphasizes recruiting the harder-to-acquire supply side, prioritizing liquidity within a narrow niche over raw volume, and giving supply independent value before demand scales.[1] Case-based platform research similarly emphasizes testing value propositions with real marquee users rather than relying on registrations alone.[2]
