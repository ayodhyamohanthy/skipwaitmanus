# Zero-Distribution Software Growth Research Plan

## Objective

Identify repeatable early-distribution mechanisms used by globally scaled software companies before they had material paid reach, then determine which mechanisms can help **skipwait.me** create trusted two-sided marketplace liquidity without spam, deceptive urgency, or coerced contact sharing.

## Research inputs to validate

| Company / category | Mechanism to investigate | Primary evidence priority | Relevance to skipwait.me |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dropbox | Double-sided referral incentive and product-led invitation loop | Company materials, SEC filing, founder interview | Incentivised invitations and measurable referral conversion |
| Calendly | Shared scheduling artifact as a recipient-facing acquisition channel | Company/founder materials, product documentation | Every useful referral action can create a lightweight shareable artifact |
| Canva | Template-led SEO and user-generated reuse | Company materials, public case studies | Role/application templates can create high-intent discovery without ads |
| Notion | Community champions, templates, and local groups | Official community materials, founder/company sources | Trusted micro-communities and peer-led onboarding |
| Slack | Team-based expansion from a small initial cohort | Founder/company sources and credible interviews | Employer/team entry can solve Referrer-side liquidity |
| Figma | Community publishing and collaborative access | Official community/product sources | Public-but-controlled opportunity sharing and social proof |
| HubSpot | Educational inbound content and free tools | Company methodology and public filings | Search demand capture for job-seeker and referrer pain points |
| Airbnb / marketplace pattern | Supply-first local liquidity and narrow-market concentration | Founders’ sources, academic or company materials | Start with dense role/company corridors rather than broad geography |

## Questions

The study will distinguish between an acquisition channel, a product loop, a retention loop, and a paid growth accelerator. It will assess activation prerequisites, marginal cost, trust risks, abuse potential, and leading indicators. It will not treat public market value as evidence that a company’s tactics are transferable.

## Exclusions

The playbook will not recommend deceptive growth hacks, unsolicited bulk outreach, scraping personal contacts, fabricated testimonials, fake job openings, incentive designs that distort referral quality, or transmission of personal documents without explicit consent.
