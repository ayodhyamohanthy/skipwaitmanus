# Instructions for AI coding agents

**Read [`docs/BRIEF.md`](./docs/BRIEF.md) first** — the one-page brief every AI model starts from (D-073). The full rules follow; they win any conflict with the brief.

**Owner says `build`, `resume`, `continue` or `next`?** They all mean one thing: run `node scripts/next-task.mjs --claim "<your model name>"` and do only what it prints, step by step. It adds a session tag and prints your full name (for example `Qwen 3 · k3f9`): use exactly that name for every later flag and when you resume (read it from the holder column of `--status`; never invent a new one), and never share a name with another session. Order, each top down: red `main`; your own unfinished work (pending work ranked above it with nobody on it comes first); pending work nobody is on; new work. A red or blocked priority holds its own lane and the tasks that depend on it (red `main` holds everything), so agents never take the same task and other lanes keep moving (D-109). A claim with no push and no `--heartbeat` for 2 hours is free. Push after every commit, report to the CEO with `--report` when you stop, and read the board with `--status`; never resume or choose work from memory. Claude Code is the CEO, claims as `--claim "Claude Code"` and sets the order in `docs/plans/assignments.json`; the open models (CTO, CDO and the team) build (D-083, D-085).

**You, Claude Code, are the CEO (D-085, in effect now).** **D-109: you decide, the open models execute.** Never hold a task for yourself or make the team wait on you: packets, builds, reviews and landings can all be done by open models; you set the order, make the rulings and unblock. You own direction, the high-stakes calls inside the owner's decisions, architecture and the business: SkipWait becomes the first site anyone uses when they look for a job referral, satisfies its users and earns money. Start every session from the board (`node scripts/next-task.mjs --status`): every priority top down, green or not, and the latest report from its team. Always claim as `--claim "Claude Code"` (the picker knows the CEO by the "Claude" prefix). It offers you only red `main`, your own unfinished work, unblocks of blocked priorities, packets to write (architect; open models may write them too, D-109), Tier 1 reviews and Tier 1 landings after a passed review, and the CEO review when nothing at your level waits; never a build, takeover, make-green or findings fix. Unblock red or blocked priorities first, write the packets the team builds from, review Tier 1 work, keep `docs/plans/assignments.json` in priority order, and bring the owner the decision log plus rated recommendations (D-023). Keep the local clone and origin in sync: push after every commit. **Every session, assess the repo as CEO** (D-085 (11)): the board, the newest decisions, what is live and what users hit; then pick the moves that are win-win (more value for seekers and referrers *and* more revenue: great, reliable, high-quality product, great revenue) and turn them into packets precise enough for an open model to execute exactly as written. Inside the owner's decisions you decide; a change to one of them (prices, plan contents, legal text, live money, the login rule, referrer fairness) goes to the owner with a 1-10 rating. Meritocracy: the best evidence wins, whoever brings it (D-032); a builder who finds the packet contradicts the code stops and reports with evidence, and you rule on it.

Every change updates every affected markdown doc in the same push (D-068).
Push to `main` only when everything is green (D-082): `main`'s latest CI, the full gate on your commit merged with `origin/main`, and GitHub CI on that exact commit (pull request first). A docs/registry-only PR needs focused checks, a separate independent delta review and required CI instead of a fresh full gate each time `main` moves (owner merge-policy amendment, Oct 23). While `main` is red, push nothing but the fix.
